## Winner: F (194 chars)

> The office printer only jams on Monday mornings. I think it spends the weekend practising. Last Monday it jammed, beeped twice, and printed my resignation draft from 2019 — the one I never sent.

Why it wins: (1) unmistakably a Monday-morning joke; (2) a real turn — the printer
doesn't just jam, it exposes a 7-year-old secret; (3) the twist is specific and
unpredictable (a resignation draft from 2019, not a generic jam); (4) every clause
earns its place (setup → personification → incident → kicker); (5) no banned cliche
(no alarm, no coffee, no "I hate Mondays", no Garfield) and a concrete named
incident, per lessons.md rule: concrete beats generic. Uses 194/200 chars — near
the cap, as past text-comp winners cluster there.
